What are these checks?

WebRTC leak: Browsers can expose your real IP address via WebRTC even through a VPN or proxy. If a local/private IP is detected, it may indicate a leak. Disable WebRTC in your browser or use an extension like uBlock Origin (enable 'Prevent WebRTC from leaking local IP addresses').

Canvas fingerprint: Sites use the HTML5 Canvas API to generate a unique identifier for your browser. If your blocker randomises or blocks canvas reads, this test will show as protected.

FAQ

How does the test work?

Clicking Run Test fires three independent checks simultaneously: (1) host-fetch tests that attempt HEAD requests to known ad/tracker domains, (2) cosmetic filter tests that check if ad-shaped DOM elements get hidden, and (3) script-load tests that probe whether ad-named scripts are blocked. Additional privacy checks test WebRTC IP exposure and canvas fingerprinting resistance. No real ads or trackers are ever loaded.

Why is my score not 100%?

Most DNS-only blockers (Pi-hole, NextDNS, ControlD) will score highly on host tests but cannot pass cosmetic filter or script blocking tests — those require a browser-level extension like uBlock Origin. A score above 70% for a DNS filter is excellent.

How do I share my results?

Click the Share button in the header after a test completes. Your score is encoded into the URL hash — no data is sent to any server.

What does the WebRTC leak check do?

It uses the browser's RTCPeerConnection API to enumerate ICE candidates, which can reveal your local or public IP address even through a VPN. If local IPs appear, consider enabling WebRTC leak protection in your VPN app or uBlock Origin's settings.

My blocker is working but the test shows failures. Why?

This test domain may not be in your blocker's rule lists. That doesn't mean your blocker is broken — it means it hasn't specifically targeted this domain. Visit a popular ad-heavy site to confirm real-world protection. You can also add the domain-specific rules shown in each failing test card.
